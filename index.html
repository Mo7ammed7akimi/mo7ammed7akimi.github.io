Mohammed Alhakimi

Doctoral Researcher · TU Clausthal

I am a doctoral researcher at TU Clausthal, working on scheduling algorithms and resource orchestration for Kubernetes and distributed systems. My research focuses on optimizing container placement, QoS-aware scheduling, and edge–cloud continuum.

With a background in Computer Science (Master, Universiti Putra Malaysia) and Information Systems (Bachelor, Future University), I combine theoretical foundations with hands-on cloud-native development.
